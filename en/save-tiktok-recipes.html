Frequently asked questions

Do I have to open the app to save a TikTok?

No. Sharing from TikTok is enough — RightSetFret processes the recipe in the background and lets you know when the card is ready.

Does it work when the amounts are only spoken?

Yes. RightSetFret listens to the spoken audio and combines it with the visuals and the caption. If something is missing, you add it in a tap.

Is saving TikTok recipes free?

Your first 10 imports are free. After that you pick a subscription with a 7-day free trial. Viewing and exporting saved recipes stays free forever.

Does RightSetFret store the video?

No. The card links to the original video and its creator; the video itself stays on TikTok.

Does it also work with TikTok, YouTube or regular websites?

Yes. RightSetFret imports from Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest, Facebook and YouTube, from any recipe website, and even from a screenshot or a photo of a cookbook or magazine. Paste the link or share the photo — the card comes out the same.
